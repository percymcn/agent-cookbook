# Claude Business Funding Framework
Source: https://youtu.be/Hk-OWK--n0g — "Claude Just Changed Business Funding Forever"

## The 6-Step Framework (from video frames)

### Step 1: Tell the Bank Who You Are
**Claude Prompt:** "Write a professional company description for my LLC [insert name]. Explain what we do, who we serve, and why we're positioned for growth. Make it funding-ready for a bank, credit union, or SBA lender."

Key elements:
- What the business does (specific, not generic)
- Who you serve (target market)
- Revenue model
- Positioning — why you'll succeed
- Make it a SELLING POINT for the bank/credit union/SBA

### Step 2: Business Description (Example output)
Claude generates a professional business description that:
- Describes services and target market
- Highlights growth trajectory
- Shows client acquisition strategy
- Demonstrates industry expertise
- Positions for continued expansion
- Mentions key differentiators

### Step 3: Show Banks Why Your Market Wins (Market Analysis)
**Claude Prompt:** "Generate a market analysis for my business in the [insert your industry]. Highlight industry growth, competitive gaps, and why my company is in a solid position for funding."

Key elements:
- Total Addressable Market (TAM) with real numbers
- Industry growth rate (CAGR)
- Competitive landscape gaps
- Why YOUR company fills those gaps
- Position as "low risk, high-growth, fundable candidate"

### Step 4: Give Banks the Numbers They Need (Financial Projections)
**Claude Prompt:** "Generate 3-year financial projections for my LLC generating [$X in year 1 revenue], [X% growth year-over-year], at [X% operating expenses]. Format in a table with revenue, expenses, and profit so the bank can read it."

Key elements:
- Revenue projections (Year 1, 2, 3)
- Expenses breakdown
- Net profit margins
- Growth trajectory showing hockey stick
- Formatted as clean table banks can read

### Step 5: Financial Projections Table Format
| Metric | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Revenue | $X | $X | $X |
| Expenses | $X | $X | $X |
| Net Profit | $X | $X | $X |
| Margin | X% | X% | X% |

"This isn't a dream — these are REALISTIC projections based on current clients, market trends, and operational efficiency."

### Step 6: Compliance & Fundability Check
**Claude Prompt:** "Run a compliance and fundability assessment for my LLC. Check for: EIN, DUNS number, registered agent, business bank account, operating agreement, state compliance. Flag anything missing."

Key elements:
- Business entity verification (LLC filing, EIN, DUNS)
- Registered agent status
- Commercial address (not PO Box)
- Business bank account (separate from personal)
- Operating Agreement on file
- State compliance (annual reports, good standing)
- Insurance (general liability, professional)
- 411 listing (phone directory presence)

## How to Apply This
1. Run each prompt for each LLC
2. Compile outputs into a single "Business Funding Package"
3. Submit to banks, credit unions, SBA lenders, and alternative lenders
4. Use Nav.com to find matching lenders
5. Apply with confidence — you have professional, data-backed documents
