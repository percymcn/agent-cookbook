### Target Customer
- Individuals with specialized knowledge/expertise in any niche
- Freelancers, consultants, coaches looking to productize their knowledge
- Tech-savvy entrepreneurs interested in AI automation
- Those wanting to create passive income streams from existing expertise
- People interested in no-code/low-code AI business models

### Solution Mechanism
1. Identify a specific, repeatable task or process from your expertise
2. Document it as a detailed SOP (Standard Operating Procedure) for AI
3. Create prompts, workflows, and instructions that Claude/Follow can execute
4. Package as a "Claude Skill" or AI automation product
