## Business Model Components

### Core Proposition
Help business owners maximize value from their existing credit card spending through strategic points optimization, consulting, and potentially affiliate/referral income from recommended cards and tools.

### Target Customer
- Business owners who use credit cards for business expenses
- Entrepreneurs and freelancers with significant monthly spend
- Individuals interested in travel hacking and points optimization
- Those looking for service-based side hustles with high ticket pricing
- People who want to leverage existing spending rather than find new revenue streams

### Solution Mechanism
1. Analyze client's current credit card usage and spending patterns
2. Recommend optimal cards for different spending categories (travel, dining, office supplies, etc.)
3. Strategize point earning and redemption for maximum value
4. Help clients avoid common mistakes (hoarding points, poor redemption choices)
5. Implement systems for tracking and optimizing ongoing spending
6. Charge consulting fees ($875 per call mentioned)
7. Potentially earn affiliate income from recommended financial products

### Revenue Model
- Consulting fees (primary: $875 per call)
- Affiliate commissions from recommended credit cards/financial products
- Potential for retainer services or ongoing optimization
- Digital products/courses on credit card optimization
- Newsletter sponsorships (Beehiiv mentioned)
- Community/membership access

### Customer Acquisition
- LinkedIn (primary source mentioned for clients)
- YouTube channel (@thekoerneroffice)
- Podcast appearances
- Referrals from satisfied clients
- Content marketing (blogs, social media)
- Beehiiv newsletter (tkopod.com)
- Private community (TKOwners.com)
- Networking and partnerships

### Delivery Method
- One-on-one consulting calls (Zoom/phone)
- Potentially group workshops or courses
- Digital delivery of analysis/recommendations
- Email follow-up and tracking systems
- Community support and Q&A
- Newsletter content delivery

### Competitive Edge
- Specific niche focus (credit card points for business owners)
- Proven results ($231K in first full year)
- High-ticket consulting model
- Leverages existing spending (no new revenue needed)
- Partnership/investment in Beehiiv adds credibility
- Focus on practical, actionable strategies

### Scalability Factors
- Consulting model limited by time (1:1)
- Digital products/courses can scale infinitely
- Affiliate income passive and scalable
- Group programs/workshops increase leverage
- Automated analysis tools could increase efficiency
- Newsletter and community create scale opportunities
