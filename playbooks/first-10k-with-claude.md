# How I'd Make My First $10K With Claude
Source: https://youtu.be/ykvcV7cET5A
Date: 2026-05-05

## Video Summary
Creator built a $15K + $15K/mo deal from a fire safety inspection company using Claude Code. Shows the exact 7-phase system to close $10K+ AI automation deals with boring local businesses.

## Key Insight: 1,000 Strangers vs 4 Business Owners
- B2C apps (habit trackers, productivity) = need thousands of users, marketing budget, network effects
- B2B services for boring businesses = need 4 clients at $2,500/mo = $10K
- Boring businesses are gold mines because:
  1. They look boring (no competition)
  2. They make real money ($500K-$10M/yr)
  3. Boring problems are easy to solve with AI

## The Boring Problems Framework
5 categories where $10K+ opportunities live:

1. **Manual Repetitive Processes** — Paper forms, copy-paste, data entry, manual scheduling
2. **Lead/Customer Response Delays** — Slow to respond, miss calls, no after-hours capability
3. **Outdated/Legacy Software** — 20-year-old systems, spreadsheets, fragmented tools
4. **Inconsistent Operations** — No SOPs, quality varies by employee, training gaps
5. **Revenue/Reporting Blind Spots** — Don't know their numbers, can't track profitability

## The 7-Phase System (All Skills Built)

| Phase | Skill | What It Does |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | fluxio-niche-research | Deep market research on a boring niche |
| 2 | fluxio-opportunity-finder | Find 3-5 specific $10K+ problems |

## How He Closed $32,500
1. Researched fire safety inspection niche
2. Found they fill inspection forms manually, miss follow-ups
3. Called company, did 15-min discovery
4. Recorded call on Google Meet, fed transcript to Claude
5. Claude generated full blueprint with components, timeline, pricing
6. Exported as PDF proposal
7. Client approved -> $32,500 deal (build + training + retainer)
